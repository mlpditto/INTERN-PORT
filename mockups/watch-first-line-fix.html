🎬 Watch first ▸ 🪄 fix one line with AI

Mockup. Click around: 🪄 · the model chips · ↩️ work (the AI answers are canned). Each fix sends only that line + its own Read first note (from its Q number), so only that line loses its voices — the rest stay green. The answer goes through the same ⚠️ number check. Default model = AI Settings, until you pick one.

A · 🪄 on every line, one model beside ✨

🪄 sits on each line (bright on ⚠️ lines, faint on the rest). Tap → the line fades ⏳ → the new text drops in, ringed violet, with ↩️ to put the old one back. The header gets 🪄 fix 3 ⚠️ for all flagged lines at once. One model picker (the chip beside ✨) serves ✨, ↻ and 🪄.

B · 🪄 opens a row under the line

Tap 🪄 → a row opens right under that line (§3.7): the model picker, quick asks (🔢 fix numbers · ✂ shorter · 💬 plainer) or your own words, then 🪄 Go. The answer shows old (struck) → new; tap the new text to use it, ↩️ keeps the old. Model can differ per line.

C · Pick lines, fix them together

Tap the Q tags of the lines you want (they turn violet) → a bar appears on top: 🪄 fix 2 lines + the model picker. One AI call for all picked lines. Each changed line gets its own ↩️.

D · Two models side by side

🪄 on a line asks two models at once (the two you pinned); both answers show under the line — tap the better one. Good to compare models; costs 2 calls a line.
