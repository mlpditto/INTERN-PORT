RUBRIC — score each question on 7 dimensions, INTEGER 1-5 (1 = poor, 5 = excellent):
  1. keyDefensibility    — Is the marked answer defensible? Judge the "correct:" line given below, not what you wish it said.

  2. clarity             — Is the stem unambiguous, well-worded, free of double-barrel?
  3. distractorQuality   — Are wrong options plausible to a learner? 1 = obvious giveaways, 5 = distractors that test real understanding
  4. itemFlaws           — Technical item-writing flaws (NBME-style). Scan for: cueing between stem and key;
                           an option the stem itself rules out ("X was already tried"); "all/none of the above";
                           the correct option being conspicuously the longest or most qualified; absolutes

                           1 = a flaw that hands the answer away, 3 = one cosmetic flaw, 5 = clean
  5. bloomLevel          — Cognitive demand of the question:
                           1 = Remember (recall facts),

                           3 = Apply (use in a new situation),

  6. clinicalDepth       — Clinical reasoning required: 1 = pure factoid, 3 = single-step clinical inference, 5 = multi-step synthesis (diagnose + plan)
  7. languageConsistency — Polish and consistency: 1 = mixed languages / typos / jargon errors, 5 = clean polished prose throughout
