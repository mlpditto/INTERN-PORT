# Intern guide — how to use the app

> Short guide — every button name comes from the real screen (open it again any time: ⚙️ **Settings ▸ 📖 Guide**)

## 1. 🏁 Start here

The top card is **you**: photo, name, points (**pts**), 🪙 Beri, level, and a bar showing how many internship days are left.

| Button (top tool rail) | What it does |
|---|---|
| 📅 **Schedule** | Your training calendar and goals. The number on the corner = items due **today** |
| ✏️ **Personal Info** | Your profile, internship dates, social handles |
| **EN** | Language — each tap cycles EN → KR → TH → K·T |
| 🌊 **Flood watch** | River / flood risk near your training site |
| ⚙️ **Settings** | Privacy preferences, reload, and **📖 Guide** (this page) |

**Do this first**
1. Open ✏️ **Personal Info** and set your **internship start / end dates** — the days-left bar and Schedule depend on them
2. Just open the app each day — **check-in is automatic** (the **Daily check-in** card under your profile shows it) and keeps your streak going
3. Tap **⋯** under the check-in card for monthly progress, certificate progress 🏅, your next level ✨ and the 🏆 **Leaderboard**

The page scrolls top to bottom: **Mission** (Quiz · Journal · Explore) → **DD Codex** → **History**. Tap a section's title bar to open or close it. The round **＋** button (bottom right) is **Submit New**.

---

## 2. 📝 Quiz

Open **Mission ▸ Quiz**. Each card is one quiz with its **deadline** — the sooner the deadline, the sooner you should do it.

- 🔍 In the **Mission** title bar: find a quiz that is not on your list, or **request** one
- **ONE-TIME quizzes** — the first run is **Practice** (no points). When you finish, tap **Request Real Exam**; the admin allows it, then you retake it for real and it counts
- Quizzes that are due soon and not done yet are the ones you want to finish first

**Daily Quiz Digest (LINE group)**
The bot posts a **Daily Quiz Digest** in the intern LINE group at **16:00**:
- ✅ **Done today** — what was submitted since yesterday
- ⏳ **Due soon** — quizzes still pending and when they close
- Tap **Open quizzes** to jump straight to the Quiz tab

---

## 3. 📓 Journal

Open **Mission ▸ Journal** and write your **daily reflection**: pick a **mood** (😊 😓 💪 🤔 …) then write what you did and learned. Thai or English — both are fine.

- **My Writing Profile** shows total words, active days, your streak, and your TH / EN balance
- The small number on the Journal tab = logs that already received an **admin bonus**
- Writing every day builds your 🔥 **streak** (shown on the History tab)

---

## 4. ➕ Submit New

The round **＋** button (bottom right) is the one door for everything you hand in:

| Type | Use it for |
|---|---|
| 🩺 **Case** | A patient case you saw (HN, short note) |
| 📄 **Work** | A piece of work — a title and a link |
| 🔗 **Explore** | Suggest a link for the Explore tab |
| 📅 **Event** | Invite your group to an event (title, date, place, join link) |
| ⋯ **Drug · Disease · Product** | Add a Codex entry or a product listing |

Everything you submit shows up in **History** with its status. Cases are also in **History ▸ 🩺 ▸ 📊 My Cases**.

---

## 5. 🏆 Points

**pts = learning points.** They set your level and your Leaderboard rank.

| You get | pts |
|---|---|
| ✅ Open the app once a day (automatic check-in) | +0.01 |
| 🔥 Every 7th day in a row | +0.05 |
| 📓 Submit a Journal | +0.1 |
| 🩺 Submit a Case | +0.1 |
| 🌅 Morning Quiz Bonus — first person each day to submit a regular quiz, 08:00–12:00 | +0.1 |
| 📝 Scored quizzes | depends on the quiz (some need admin approval) |
| ⭐ Admin bonus (good Journals / Cases) | admin's call |

- **Level** goes up every 10 pts: LV.2 at 10 · LV.3 at 20 … LV.10 at 90. The next level is under **⋯**
- 🏅 Certificate progress and the 🏆 Leaderboard are under **⋯** too

---

## 6. 🪙 Beri

**Beri = collectible coins.** Separate from pts (they do **not** count towards level or Leaderboard); you spend them in the shop.

| You get | Beri |
|---|---|
| 🧭 Open a link in **Explore** for the first time | the earlier you open it, the more (drops by 1 for each person) |
| ✍️ A link review the admin approves | +1 |
| 🥇🥈🥉 First 3 to submit a quiz | 10 · 6 · 4 |
| ⏱️ Submit a quiz ≥48 / ≥24 / ≥6 h before the deadline | 5 · 3 · 1 |
| ⭐ Special gift from the admin | admin's call |

- Quiz numbers are defaults; the admin can change or switch them off per quiz, and you get them when the admin approves the quiz
- Once you hold **more than 500 Beri**, the **Murthehelp** shop opens (and stays open even if your Beri drops)
- Your balance is the 🪙 under your name on the profile card

---

## 7. 💊 Codex

**DD Codex** — a shared library of **💊 Drugs** and **🩺 Diseases** written by interns and approved by the admin.
- Search and open an entry; some entries have files you can download in one tap
- Want to add one? **＋ ▸ ⋯ ▸ Drug / Disease**. Your submissions wait in **📝 My Drafts** until approved

**Explore** (Mission ▸ Explore) — links picked by the admin (shops, videos…).
- Opening a link for the first time earns **🪙 Beri** — the earlier you open it, the more you get
- Spend Beri in the Beri shop; write a short review on links you used
